a short reflection on the recruiting cycle, and how much of what we chase during
it is borrowed — the companies everyone wants, the roles that signal status, the
timelines we measure ourselves against.

rené girard called this mimetic desire: we want things not for themselves, but
because other people want them. recruiting season is a near-perfect case study.
